A Mechanically-Linked Duo

Trick & Treat — a lure-chain pair, shown on one wide map with the path between them.

Trick & Treat

Large Fish · Halloween Pair
Large FishHard
🎯 Cast:  Hook Trick above the Reef wall on Halloween night, then lead it across to the surface above the Silo.
Reef wall (Trick) — dashed lure path — Above Silo (Treat)

About

Trick only appears on Halloween night — set your clock to 12:00 AM, October 31st, and watch for the "Happy Halloween!" prompt. Treat doesn't exist at all until you personally deliver Trick to the right patch of water above the Silo.

Trick

A seasonal fish that only exists on Halloween night. Found just above the Coral Reef wall once the clock strikes midnight on October 31st — the game marks the night with a "Happy Halloween!" pop-up if you're not sure it registered.

🪱 Bait: Medium📅 Timing: Oct 31, 12 AM

Treat

Doesn't spawn on its own. Treat only appears in the surface water above the Silo, and only after Trick has been personally delivered there — a ghostly reward for finishing the trip.

🪱 Bait: none, auto-spawns📍 Requires: Trick delivered first

Special Instructions

Catch Trick with medium bait, but don't bank it yet. Lure it the whole way across the map to the upper sea above the Silo — right where your sonar zooms in, under the big surface cluster. Treat only appears once Trick arrives there. Clearing out the larger surface fish first makes the trip a lot less chaotic.

Gear Up (shared)

  • 🪱 Medium bait required
  • 🪝 Flick attachment
  • 📅 Date set to Oct 31, 12:00 AM required

C Solo Feature

Underfin — a secret fish with a mechanic too involved to share a page.

Underfin

Secret Fish · The Empty
Secret FishHard
🎯 Cast:  Bottom of The Empty cave, hugging the Cuddlecanth fossil.
Fossil corner (start) → drag right → Underfin bites

About

A sleek, all-black fish hiding beneath the map itself, at the very bottom of The Empty cave. It doesn't strike at a normal drop — it has to be coaxed out with a dragging motion along the cave floor.

Special Instructions

Hook a small fish on medium bait with a huge hook and Flick equipped. Lower it beside the Cuddlecanth fossil, hugging the left wall near the very bottom of the map. Without reeling up, move your boat left until your sonar shows the line near the far-left corner, then Flick right to free the bait toward the cave's center. If your bait settles mid-cave, you should hear Underfin take the small fish. Re-try if it snaps on the rock instead.

Gear Up

  • 🪱 Medium bait + small fish required
  • 🪝 Huge hook required
  • 🎣 Flick attachment required
🐟 Category: Secret Fish📍 Zone: The Empty, floor👁️ Spotted: rarely, easy to miss entirely
